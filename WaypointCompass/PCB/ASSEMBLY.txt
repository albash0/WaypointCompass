WAYPOINT COMPASS CARRIER — REV B
KiCad 10 project: WaypointCompassCarrier.kicad_pro

This is a 60 x 40 mm, two-layer carrier. All assembled parts on the carrier
are through-hole pin headers, two tactile buttons, and one removable jumper shunt.
Power comes from the ESP32-S3 SuperMini's USB connector.

DIRECT HEADERS AND CONNECTORS

Reference  Side   Connector                    Purpose
J1         Back   Two 1x9 male headers         ESP32-S3 SuperMini
J2         Front  One 1x7 male header          Round GC9A01 TFT
J3         Back   One 1x4 male header          GPS cable
J4         Back   One 1x8 male header          GY-521 / MPU-6050
J5         Back   One 1x5 male header          GY-271 / QMC5883P
JP1        Back   One 1x3 male header + shunt  GPS supply selection
SW1        Front  Standard 6x6 tactile switch GPIO1 input, pressed = GND
SW2        Front  Standard 6x6 tactile switch GPIO2 input, pressed = GND

All connectors have 2.54 mm pitch and 1.00 mm plated holes. J1 uses
15.24 mm between the two header rows, with 20.32 mm between end pins.
Use straight male headers soldered into BOTH the module and carrier PCBs.
The plastic spacer sits between the boards and sets a 2.54 mm gap between
the facing PCB surfaces. There are no female sockets on J1/J2/J4/J5.
Choose pins long enough for both PCB thicknesses, the spacer, and solder
protrusion on both outer faces (the model uses an 8.4 mm total pin length).

PIN MAPS

The following orders describe each module's own printed header order,
for the module prototype. Pin 1 is the square carrier pad.

J2 TFT:     1 VCC, 2 GND, 3 SCL/CLK, 4 SDA/DIN, 5 DC, 6 CS, 7 RST.
J3 GPS:     1 VCC, 2 RX, 3 TX, 4 GND.
J4 GY-521:  1 VCC, 2 GND, 3 SCL, 4 SDA, 5 XDA, 6 XCL, 7 AD0, 8 INT.
J5 GY-271:  1 DRDY (label supplied as DD0Y), 2 SDA, 3 SCL, 4 GND, 5 VCC.

J1 SuperMini, viewed from its component side with USB at the top:
  Left row, top to bottom:  TX, RX, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.
  Right row, top to bottom: 5V, GND, 3V3, 13, 12, 11, 10, 9, 8.
This follows the existing SuperMini footprint in the original module prototype.

WORKING CIRCUIT CONNECTIONS

TFT SCL/CLK -> GPIO10; SDA/DIN -> GPIO11; DC -> GPIO13.
TFT RST -> 3V3; CS -> GND; VCC -> 3V3. GPIO6 and GPIO12 are unused.
GY-521 SDA -> GPIO8; SCL -> GPIO9; VCC -> 3V3; AD0 -> GND (address 0x68).
GY-271 SDA -> GY-521 XDA; SCL -> GY-521 XCL; VCC -> 3V3.
The existing firmware enables the MPU-6050's auxiliary I2C bypass.
GPS TX -> ESP32 RX/GPIO44; GPS RX -> ESP32 TX/GPIO43.
GY-521 INT and GY-271 DRDY are unconnected; firmware polls the sensors.
SW1 connects GPIO1 to GND when pressed; SW2 connects GPIO2 to GND.
These inputs need INPUT_PULLUP and software debounce in any future button
handler. Button actions are not yet assigned in the current firmware.

JP1 has 3V3 on pin 1, GPS VCC on pin 2, and USB 5V on pin 3.
Fit ONE shunt across 1–2 for a 3.3 V supply, or across 2–3 for a 5 V supply,
according to your GPS breakout's VCC rating. Without the shunt, GPS is off.
The UART signals remain at 3.3 V regardless of the selected GPS supply.

MECHANICAL FIT AND ASSEMBLY

Inspect the 60 x 40 mm native PCB at 1:1 scale and verify the actual module headers before ordering.

The TFT outline follows the supplied 38 x 45.5 mm drawing. Its round screen
is centered at (30, 20) mm on the 60 x 40 mm carrier. It is rotated
90 degrees so the 45.5 mm dimension runs along the carrier's 60 mm side.
Two 2.00 mm unplated holes match the TFT drawing's support holes, with
19.26 mm between centers. Use suitable M1.6 nonmagnetic hardware and
2.54 mm standoffs between the facing PCB surfaces without bending the TFT.
Do not use the reference STEP mounting holes to choose carrier drilling.
Four 2.20 mm corner holes are available for M2 case mounting.
The drawing's printed order RST CS DC SDA SCL GND VCC runs from pin 7 to
pin 1; the carrier's square VCC pad matches the TFT drawing's pin-1 marker.

The two 6 x 6 mm buttons sit on the front, outside the display outline.
Viewed from the display side, with the board's top-left corner at (0, 0),
SW1's body center is (5, 11.5) mm and SW2's is (5, 27) mm. There is 3 mm
between the buttons' right body edges and the TFT's leftmost PCB edge.
Their footprint is KiCad's standard SW_PUSH_6mm: four leads arranged
6.50 x 4.50 mm, 1.10 mm plated drills and 2.00 mm copper pads. The two
pad-1 leads are internally common; the two pad-2 leads are internally common.
Verify this lead spacing on your switches before purchasing or ordering.
The generic switch model has a short actuator (about 4.3 mm overall height).
Since the TFT sits above its header spacer, provide case plungers if your enclosure
requires the buttons to be reached from the same height as the screen face.
The SuperMini moved 4 mm right and the GY-521 moved 0.5 mm right to clear
the button leads; the display center and carrier dimensions are unchanged.

The GY-521 body envelope is nominally 20 x 16 mm and the GY-271 envelope
15 x 18 mm. These module body dimensions have not been measured on your
parts. Their header pin counts/order are supplied by you; 2.54 mm sensor
header pitch is assumed. Check the fit print, especially these two bodies.
Supplier module reference solids and derived assembly STEPs are omitted from the public export. Check actual module dimensions and fit.

The reference ESP32 has 2.5019 mm pitch / 15.0368 mm row spacing; the carrier
retains 2.54 / 15.24 mm. The GY-271 reference has 2.60 mm pitch; the carrier
retains 2.54 mm. TFT reference support holes are 18.86 mm apart and its pin
row is 24.996 mm from the round PCB center; the supplied drawing and carrier
use 19.26 mm and 24.74 mm. The drawing-defined display remains centered.
These differences are also listed in module_models.json. The STEP assembly
is useful for visualization and case planning, but does not certify fit.
The reference GY-521 body extends slightly beyond its nominal Fab outline
and approaches the ESP32 and JP1 closely; check your actual boards, jumper,
and support hardware at the chosen 2.54 mm gap using the fit prints.

Solder the headers into the modules with their plastic spacers on the side
that will face the carrier. With all boards unpowered, hold the modules and
carrier parallel in a fixture and solder the free pin ends into the carrier.
Install the rear modules, GPS/selector headers and front switches before the
front TFT, since the TFT covers some carrier solder joints. Trim protruding
ends so pins and solder cannot touch the opposing module within the gap.
Support the TFT mechanically; its header alone should not carry button/case
loads. Match the square pad and physical pin labels before soldering, since
these modules cannot be unplugged after direct mounting. The USB connector
and BOOT button remain accessible from the back.

The FRONT < mark points along the carrier's long axis toward its left edge
when looking at the TFT/front face. This is also the display's native up
direction with the existing tft.setRotation(0). Verify the GY-521 and GY-271
printed sensor axes against your working assembly; a changed mounting
orientation needs the corresponding firmware sensor-frame adjustment.
After mounting everything, press BOOT and perform a new all-axis 10-second
calibration. The assembled board's magnetic offsets can differ from the
breadboard's saved calibration. Keep steel hardware away from the GY-271.

Order a standard 2-layer FR-4 board, 60 x 40 mm, 1.6 mm thick, 1 oz copper.
The signal traces are 0.25 mm; power traces are 0.40 mm. Minimum clearance
is 0.20 mm. Vias are 0.70 mm with 0.30 mm plated drills. Through-hole header
pads are 1.80 mm diameter with 1.00 mm plated drills. Switch pads have
2.00 mm diameter and 1.10 mm plated drills. No stencil is required.

KiCad electrical checks and physical checks passed with zero reported
violations, zero missing connections, and zero schematic/PCB differences.
This is a CAD-verified design; an assembled PCB has not yet been tested.
The existing firmware and the original EspCompass project were preserved.
